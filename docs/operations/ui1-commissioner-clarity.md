## Controls, consent and privacy

Recovery contains existing manual odds/score checks, eligible preparation/opening,
finalization, corrections, postponement actions and guarded automation retry.
Season settings contains approval, pause/resume/revoke, league management and
completed setup. Deep links open their containing disclosures without acquiring
provider data or invoking commands. Existing server/database guards and explicit
confirmations remain authoritative. Collapsing content does not reduce queries.

A missing individual player is different from absent structural slate/source
readiness. Known player counts are labeled selected/published, not guaranteed
executable bets. Whole unavailable menus remain valid only under existing gates.
Manual scopes retain genuine menu review. The commissioner-only status RPC is
never exposed to members; member playoff guidance is conditional when enrollment
cannot be read. Commissioner playoff guidance uses actual approved scope.

Pause affects covered future preparation/publication. Current quotes, scores,
player results and approved pending props continue. Resume preserves approval;
revocation requires renewed genuine consent. No change to rule packages,
eligibility, receipts, result authority, hidden picks, budgets or polling.
