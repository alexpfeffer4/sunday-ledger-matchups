**Subject:** NFL data use and retention clarification — Sunday Ledger

Hello API-Sports team,

I am preparing Sunday Ledger (ledgerleagues.com), a small, private, invite-only

virtual credits with no cash value; there are no payments, purchased credits or
prizes.

We are considering API-NFL on the free plan to obtain player identities, final

authenticated league matchup and history pages.

Could you clarify whether this use is permitted, including:

1. Displaying player names, final passing/rushing/receiving yardage, verified

2. Permanently retaining the minimum normalized settlement evidence needed to

league history and auditable corrections, rather than a general sports-data
redistribution service.

Please identify any applicable publication or fantasy-use permission, required
attribution, retention restriction, fee or third-party rights approval. If
another rights holder must authorize this use, please identify the appropriate
organization and official contact route. Please also let us know if the free
