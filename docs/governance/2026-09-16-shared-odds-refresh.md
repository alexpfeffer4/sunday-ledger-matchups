# Shared automatic odds refresh — Version 1.0

The September 16 owner handoff authorizes one integrated implementation, isolated
verification, PR and fixture Preview. Production migration, deployment, budget
changes and activation require approval of the exact tested release package.

A global operational control refreshes the union of eligible published Live
coverage and applies shared public prices independently to each league event.
Targets: MAIN hourly / every 15 minutes within six hours; published prop families
every six hours / hourly within 24 hours. Authoritative entry closure wins.
Source readiness, budget, backoff and worker capacity may delay these targets.
Future schedule rows are forecast inputs only. PLANNED preparation remains with
existing season automation; paused season enrollment does not pause open-week
quotes. No weekly commissioner click or new season enrollment is introduced.

Player identity publication stays with the existing progressive authority. Frozen
players, accepted receipts, Week 2, Week 3 season consent, rules, scoring and
results are unchanged. Browsing reads stored prices; it never buys a quote.
Timestamp-only snapshots do not change economic consent. Changed lines, odds
(including improvements), withdrawals and invalid selections still require review.
The 60-second reuse, 120-second fetch validity, ten-minute observation limit,
30-second proof and one-renewal Submit safeguards remain authoritative.

The proposed upper limits are 18,000/month and 2,000/day overall, with included
background ceilings of 13,500/month and 1,300/day. Existing protected core capacity
of at least 350/day and 2,000/month remains; optional work also preserves at least
2,000 real provider-cycle credits plus forecast essential/demand headroom.
Installation is dormant and never resets counters or provider balance evidence.
See the [release package](../operations/shared-odds-refresh.md).
