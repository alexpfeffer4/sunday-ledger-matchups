# Season automation under standing policy — Version 2.2

The owner's Version 2.2 handoff supersedes the earlier architecture and prompt,
including weekly human props review for explicitly enrolled future weeks.
This amendment authorizes the implementation; Production installation, release
activation and named season enrollment require the final scoped rollout approval.

## One approval, exact weekly evidence

The current authenticated commissioner may enroll an already-started Live season,
from a chosen unopened week, in either all NFL games (recommended) or standard
Sunday afternoon onward plus Monday. Preparation begins Tuesday at 08:00 and
opening at 10:00 America/New_York, after the preceding week is FINAL. The approval
covers those settings, automatic postseason publication and the immutable
`SEASON_AUTOMATION_V1` props policy. It does not enroll another season or authorize
a material policy change. Pausing and resuming preserve the approval; revocation
invalidates uncommitted work. Explicit renewal creates a new human consent.

Each week receives a separate SYSTEM validation containing its exact menu,
source evidence, standing consent, policy hash and competitive rules identity.
No routine weekly commissioner review, props confirmation or opening click is
required. A proposal/evidence change triggers automatic revalidation. Invalid,
ambiguous, tied or stale candidates stay unavailable. Every selected official
game and all six structural slots remain mandatory. A wholly unavailable menu
is valid when source and system readiness and the complete slate are established.

Selection remains `NFLVERSE_PRIMARY` / `FEATURED_HIGHEST_STANDARD_LINES`, with
verified identities and the highest standard DraftKings QB passing, RB rushing
and WR/TE receiving lines. First publication freezes the player. The exact
initially empty slots may receive their first verified player automatically
before their own game's cutoff. Those additions use the existing acquisition,
cache, budgets, evidence, retry cadence and immutable publication authority.
Lifecycle pause/retry limits never disable an already-open week's approved
pending-slot continuation or accepted-result processing.

## Competitive identity and historical scope

Ruleset 1.5 / Product Bible 3.4 already encode these competitive selection and
publication rules, without prescribing a human weekly actor. Their canonical
bytes and hashes do not change. This operational consent amendment supplies the
new SYSTEM authorization representation. Global rules defaults, old package
versions, opened/completed bindings and accepted receipts remain unchanged.

Week 2's actual human review, reset and cutover remain actual historical events.
No new reset or replacement of those audits is authorized. Historical manual
scopes still require real review; SYSTEM validation never pretends a human
reviewed names. Nullable human audit actors are permitted only with a fenced
SYSTEM run and explicit SYSTEM provenance. Participant roles cannot invoke
internal lifecycle functions or service worker completion.

## Lifecycle and control boundaries

Canonical manual and scheduled commands share publication, finality and archive
authorities and the season lock. Staging creates no member cards or credit grant;
opening atomically validates, freezes and allocates once. Existing qualification,
review periods, Week 17 correction protections, Week 18 pairing replacement and
terminal archive lineage remain authoritative. No concurrent competitive weeks
or early betting exposure are introduced.

An existing five-minute dispatcher carries one bounded action per due season,
with a small fair batch. Failed attempts retry after 5, 15 and 60 minutes, then
suspend until relevant stored dependencies change or the commissioner requests a
guarded retry. The protected worker accepts no caller-selected scope or URL.

This amendment takes precedence over the weekly-review passages in the
[progressive decision](2026-09-15-progressive-player-props.md) and its
[runbook](../operations/progressive-player-props.md) only for enrolled future
weeks. All unrelated requirements remain effective.
